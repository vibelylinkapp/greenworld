Returns and Refund Policy

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase from Green World Health Solutions. This policy explains when and how you can return items.

Eligibility

  • Items must be unused, in their original sealed packaging, and returned within 7 days of delivery.
  • For reasons of health and safety, opened or used health and wellness products cannot be returned unless they are faulty or were incorrectly supplied.

Faulty or incorrect items

If you receive a damaged, faulty or incorrect item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will arrange a replacement or a refund at no extra cost.

How to request a return

Email info@greenworldhealth.co.ke or message 0723 579 873 with your order number and the reason for the return. We will confirm the next steps and the return address.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method, usually by M-Pesa or bank transfer, within a few business days of us receiving the returned item.
