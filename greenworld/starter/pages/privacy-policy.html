Privacy Policy

Green World Health Solutions ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website and related services.

Information we collect

  • Details you provide: name, phone number, email address, delivery address and order information when you register, place an order, subscribe or contact us.
  • Health consultation information: any health concern you choose to share when you request a consultation. This is treated as sensitive and handled with care.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as pages visited, collected to keep the site secure and improve your experience.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders and provide customer support.
  • To respond to your enquiries and health consultation requests.
  • To send updates and offers where you have asked to receive them.
  • To protect the security and integrity of our store.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with service providers who help us operate the store, such as delivery partners and payment providers including M-Pesa, and only as needed to fulfil your order or where required by law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may unsubscribe from marketing at any time. To make a request, contact us at info@greenworldhealth.co.ke.
