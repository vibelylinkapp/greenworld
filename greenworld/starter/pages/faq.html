Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order

Browse the shop, add items to your cart and check out. You can order as a guest or create an account for faster checkout and order tracking.

What payment methods do you accept

We accept M-Pesa, bank transfer and cash on delivery where available.

Do you deliver to my area

We deliver across Kenya. Nairobi orders are usually delivered same or next day, and the rest of the country within one to four business days. See our Shipping and Delivery page.

How do I track my order

Use our Track Your Order page, or contact us and we will update you.

Can I get advice on which product to choose

Yes. Request a free health consultation and our team will recommend suitable products. This guidance is general and is not a medical diagnosis.
