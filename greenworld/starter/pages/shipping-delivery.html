Shipping and Delivery

We deliver quality health and wellness products across Kenya. This page explains how delivery works, estimated timelines and costs.

Delivery areas and timelines

  • Nairobi: same day or next day delivery for orders confirmed during business hours.
  • Rest of Kenya: typically one to four business days, depending on your location and courier schedules.

Delivery charges

Delivery charges are calculated at checkout based on your location and order size. Where a promotion applies, any free-delivery threshold will be shown at checkout.

How your order is processed

  1. Place your order and pay by M-Pesa or bank transfer, or select cash on delivery where available.
  2. We confirm your order and prepare it for dispatch.
  3. You receive your items and can follow progress on our Track Your Order page.

Need help

If your order is delayed or you have a delivery question, contact us at info@greenworldhealth.co.ke or 0723 579 873, Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM.
